# 🌫️ Greyfen - The Town That Endures the Fog

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Greyfen is a **low, mist-choked town** built at the edge of wetlands and slow-moving waters. It is a place where the ground never fully dries, where fog rolls in without warning, and where people learn early that survival depends on **patience and caution** rather than strength.

Stone is rare here.  
Wood, peat, and weathered timber define the town.

Greyfen does not grow quickly—but it **does not die easily**.

---

## 🏘️ The Town Itself

Greyfen is laid out on slightly raised ground, connected by:

### Infrastructure

- Wooden walkways and low stone causeways
- Narrow bridges spanning black water channels
- Reed-lined roads marked by lantern posts

### Architecture

Buildings are squat and reinforced against damp and rot, with steep roofs and sealed shutters. Moss and lichen are everywhere, carefully trimmed rather than removed.

**At Night**: Lanterns burn low and close together—fog swallows light quickly here.

---

## ⚖️ Governance and Law

Greyfen has:

### Security Forces

- **No standing army**
- **A small detachment of sworn knights**
- A watch trained to deal with:
  - Smugglers
  - Bog-creatures
  - Undead that wander up from forgotten ground

### Response to Threats

For serious threats, Greyfen relies heavily on:
- Guild contracts
- Aid from nearby towns
- Quiet cooperation with Raven's Reach

**Attitude**: People here do not panic easily—they assume danger is part of life.

---

## 🧑‍⚖️ Mayor Elric Hallowmere

### Public Reputation

Mayor Elric Hallowmere is a **measured, deeply practical leader**, trusted not because he inspires hope, but because he tells the truth.

**Appearance**:
- Older, broad-shouldered, always bundled in layered coats
- Keeps ledgers personally rather than delegating
- Knows every family by name and history

**Background**: Born in Greyfen and has never tried to leave.

### 🧠 Leadership Style

Elric governs with:
- Careful preparation
- Reluctant honesty
- No tolerance for needless heroics

**Philosophy**:
> *"The swamp teaches you what lasts. Loud things don't."*

**Approach**:
- Authorizes guild contracts early rather than late
- Evacuates quietly when needed
- Pays attention to patterns others dismiss

---

## 🕯️ Faith in Greyfen

Shrines here are small and weathered.

### Worship

Gods honored:
- Protection
- Endurance
- Passage and death

**Offerings**: Practical items—oil, rope, iron nails

**Nature of Faith**: Not loud—**habitual**.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Greyfen

Greyfen is quiet, watchful, and tired.

### Daily Life

- People speak softly in fog
- Doors are barred early
- Outsiders are welcomed—but watched

### Common Saying

> *"If the mist comes in wrong, stay inside."*

---

## 🔮 Narrative Role

Greyfen works best as:
- An early warning town
- A place where subtle horror appears first
- A grounded contrast to grand cities and holy sites
- A base for investigations into undead, cultists, or strange phenomena

Mayor Elric Hallowmere is the kind of man who:
- Believes the party before others do
- Helps without fanfare
- Survives longer than expected—unless the fog finally wins
