# 🛤️ Marban - The Town That Holds the Line

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Marban is a **hard-edged frontier town**, built where trade roads thin out and civilization begins to fray. It is neither prosperous nor poor, neither safe nor lawless. Marban exists to **endure traffic, tension, and trouble**—and to keep them from spilling deeper into the kingdom.

> If Edmaris governs and Dreadreach watches, **Marban absorbs**.

---

## 🏘️ The Shape of the Town

Marban is compact and defensive by necessity.

### Fortifications

- Low stone walls reinforced with timber palisades
- Two main gates controlling road access
- Narrow streets designed to funnel movement
- A central square doubling as market and mustering ground

### Architecture

Buildings are practical:
- Stone lower levels
- Timber upper stories
- Reinforced shutters and barred doors

**Design Philosophy**: Nothing here is ornamental. Everything is replaceable.

---

## 🧭 Trade and Survival

Marban thrives on **passing traffic**, not production.

### Who Passes Through

- Caravans stop to rest, resupply, or regroup
- Mercenaries, traders, pilgrims, and refugees all pass through
- Goods from Millford, Ironpass, and Fellroot are redistributed here

### Economic Foundation

The town's economy depends on:
- Inns and stables
- Repair shops and smithies
- Caravan guards and guides

**Reality**: When roads become unsafe, Marban feels it first.

---

## ⚖️ Law and Order

Marban has:

### Security

- A small but experienced town guard
- A few sworn knights responsible for justice
- Temporary militias raised during spikes in danger

### Justice System

Law here is **direct and visible**.

Punishments are swift, public, and meant to discourage escalation rather than exact revenge.

### Guild Presence

Guilds operate in Marban frequently, often using it as:
- A staging ground
- A last safe stop before dangerous territory

---

## 🧑‍⚖️ Mayor Corvin Dask

### 🧔 Public Reputation

Mayor Corvin Dask is a **sharp-eyed, practical leader**, better at reading people than books.

**Appearance**:
- Lean, dark-haired, perpetually tired
- Dresses plainly, often indistinguishable from a merchant
- Keeps his office open to the square rather than hidden behind walls

**Skill**: Knows most trouble before it announces itself.

### 🧠 Governing Style

Corvin Dask governs with **situational judgment**.

**Approach**:
- Strict when needed
- Flexible when it keeps the peace
- Ruthless with repeat offenders

**Philosophy**:
> *"You don't stop chaos. You redirect it."*

**Preferences**: Prefers bribes refused, compromises honored, and violence contained.

---

## ⛪ Faith in Marban

Shrines here are pragmatic and well-used.

### Common Worship

- Gods of travel, protection, and justice are most common
- Offerings are practical: coin, oil, weapons left overnight
- Public prayer happens before departures, not festivals

**Nature of Faith**: Faith here is about **getting through the next mile**.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Marban

Marban feels:
- Crowded
- Wary
- Constantly on the edge of trouble

People don't linger unless they must.

### Common Saying

> *"If you're staying, you're waiting for something."*

---

## 🔮 Narrative Role

Marban works best as:
- A place where threats become visible before they are named
- A staging area for dangerous expeditions (Ashen Hollow lies south)
- A town that cracks before it breaks
- A crossroads where different factions and rumors collide

**Strategic Reality**: If Marban falls, the roads fall with it.
