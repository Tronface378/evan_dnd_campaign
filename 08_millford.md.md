# 🌾 Millford - The Breadbasket of the Kingdom

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Millford is not large, nor is it grand—but it is **essential**.

Built amid wide, fertile plains cut by slow rivers and irrigation channels, Millford is the **primary food producer for the Kingdom of Edmaris**. If the kingdom eats, it is because Millford worked.

Where other towns measure wealth in coin or stone, Millford measures it in **full granaries and predictable harvests**.

---

## 🚜 The Land and the Town

Millford spreads outward rather than upward.

### The Landscape

- Vast fields of grain stretch to the horizon
- Orchards and vegetable plots ring the town
- Irrigation canals and millstreams crisscross the land
- Windmills and watermills dominate the skyline

### The Town Itself

The town itself is modest:
- Timber and stone homes
- Broad barns and granaries
- Storehouses raised high to keep grain dry and safe

**Design Philosophy**: Everything here is built for **efficiency and endurance**, not beauty.

---

## ⚙️ Mills, Granaries, and Trade

Millford's mills are its lifeblood.

### Operations

- Dozens of watermills grind grain day and night
- Granaries are fortified and guarded
- Grain caravans leave on strict schedules toward:
  - Edmaris
  - Bluehaven
  - Dreadreach

### Strategic Importance

Disrupting Millford's output is considered a **national security threat**.

For this reason:
- Roads are well maintained
- Guards patrol constantly
- Guild contracts for protection are common

---

## ⚖️ Law and Defense

Millford maintains:

### Security Forces

- A small standing guard
- Several sworn knights
- A large, well-organized militia drawn from farmers and millhands

### Mobilization

In times of danger, Millford does not panic—it **mobilizes**.

Every able-bodied citizen knows:
- Where to report
- What to protect
- Which stores must never fall

---

## 🧑‍🌾 The People of Millford

Millford's people are:
- Hardworking
- Practical
- Quietly proud

### Daily Life

They rise early, work long, and speak plainly.

### Core Belief

> *"Hunger breaks kingdoms faster than swords."*

There is little patience here for politics, but deep respect for responsibility.

---

## ⛪ Faith and Daily Life

Shrines in Millford honor gods of:
- Harvest
- Weather
- Endurance
- Home and family

### Festivals

Festivals are seasonal and communal, not lavish:
- Planting rites
- Harvest feasts
- Blessings of the mills

**Nature of Faith**: Faith here is woven into routine rather than spectacle.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Millford

Millford feels:
- Busy
- Steady
- Quietly tense

As monster attacks increase elsewhere, Millford works harder, stockpiling grain and reinforcing barns.

The people know:

> *If Millford fails, the kingdom starves.*

---

## 🌾 Lord Thane Alderwick

**Titles**: Grainwarden of the Plains, Keeper of the Granaries, Steward of the Harvest Roads

### 🧔 Appearance & Bearing

Lord Thane Alderwick looks more like a prosperous landholder than a court noble.

**Physical Description**:
- Broad-shouldered, weathered, usually dressed in practical wool and leather
- Hands calloused from years spent working alongside farmers
- Keeps his hair tied back, beard neatly trimmed
- Rarely seen without a ledger or harvest tally nearby

**Demeanor**: Carries himself with **quiet authority**, not command presence.

### ⚖️ Rule and Responsibility

Alderwick governs Millford with a single guiding principle:

> *"People eat first. Pride comes later."*

**Focus Areas**:
- Maintaining stable harvests
- Protecting grain reserves
- Ensuring fair distribution during shortages

**Priorities**: Has little interest in politics beyond what threatens food supply. Court intrigue bores him; famine terrifies him.

### 🏗️ Control of the Breadbasket

Lord Alderwick personally oversees:
- Granary inspections
- Mill output schedules
- Caravan departure logs

**Secret Measures**: Maintains **emergency grain reserves** hidden across the countryside, unknown even to most of his own staff—a precaution born from old wars and bad winters.

**Policy**: Sabotage of crops or mills under his rule is treated as **treason**.

### ⚔️ Defense and Preparedness

Though Millford is not a military power, Alderwick ensures it is never helpless.

**Measures**:
- Farmers train regularly as militia
- Granaries are reinforced like fortresses
- Roads are patrolled constantly during harvest season

**Coordination**: Works closely with:
- Edmaris' quartermasters
- Nearby lords
- Guild escorts for high-risk shipments

**Response**: When danger comes, Millford closes ranks quickly.

### ⛪ Faith and Outlook

Alderwick is devout in a **practical way**.

**Practices**:
- Honors gods of harvest, endurance, and protection
- Holds public blessings before planting and reaping
- Believes the gods help those who prepare

**Expectations**: Does not expect miracles. He expects work.

### 🌫️ The Mood Around Him

Under Lord Alderwick's rule, Millford feels:
- Stable
- Busy
- Watchful

The people trust him because he never promises abundance—only **enough**.

---

## 🔮 Narrative Role

Millford works best as:
- A hub for escort missions and defense contracts
- A moral reminder of what the heroes are protecting
- A pressure point for sabotage or famine plots
- A place that represents ordinary people's survival

Lord Thane Alderwick is:
- A steady ally who values results over heroics
- A key figure when famine, war, or sabotage threatens
- Willing to sacrifice prestige, wealth, and even political standing to keep the kingdom fed

> If Millford must choose between law and survival, Alderwick will choose survival—and live with the consequences.
