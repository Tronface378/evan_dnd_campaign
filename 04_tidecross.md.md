# 🌊 Tidecross - Where the Kingdom Meets the World

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Tidecross is the **largest and busiest port city in the Kingdom of Edmaris**, a sprawling coastal metropolis built where river, road, and sea converge. It is loud, crowded, wealthy, and endlessly moving—a city that never fully sleeps, because the tide never truly rests.

> If Edmaris rules by law and Bluehaven by contract, **Tidecross rules by flow**.

Goods, people, rumors, and secrets all pass through here.

---

## ⚓ The Harbor of a Thousand Keels

Tidecross's harbor is vast and complex, shaped by centuries of expansion.

### Harbor Features

- Multiple harbors and breakwaters protect against storms
- Long stone quays packed with merchant vessels, fishing ships, and foreign traders
- Constant movement of cranes, winches, and cargo teams
- River barges arriving daily from inland towns

Ships from distant lands dock beside local fishing boats, and it is said you can hear **every language spoken somewhere on the docks**.

---

## 🏙️ The City Above the Water

Tidecross rises outward and upward from the harbor in dense layers.

### City Districts

**Dock Districts**
- Warehouses
- Taverns and sailor establishments
- Guild halls for maritime trades

**Merchant Quarters**
- Counting houses
- Wealthy estates
- Trading consortiums

**Residential Districts**
- Stacked tightly along winding streets
- Mixed economic levels
- Dense population

**Hilltop Administrative Zone**
- Towers and halls overlooking the sea
- Government offices
- Lord Admiral's command center

### The Name

Bridges span canals and river mouths, giving the city its name—**Tidecross**, where waters intersect.

---

## 💰 Trade, Coin, and Influence

Tidecross is the **economic engine of the kingdom**.

### Trade Dominance

Handles the majority of:
- Imports from foreign lands
- Exports of kingdom goods
- Foreign trade negotiations

### Power Structures

- Home to powerful merchant guilds and shipping consortiums
- Coin flows faster here than law can follow
- Where Bluehaven trades carefully, Tidecross trades **boldly**

### Dark Undercurrents

- Smuggling operations
- Foreign espionage
- Political maneuvering
- Information brokering

---

## ⚖️ Law and Order

### Security Forces

Tidecross maintains:
- A large city watch divided by districts
- Harbor guards trained for boarding actions
- Naval patrols protecting nearby waters

### Reality of Enforcement

- Law enforcement is visible but stretched thin
- Authority here is **negotiated as often as enforced**
- Guilds wield significant influence—sometimes more than the watch
- Favors can carry more weight than warrants

---

## 👑 Lord Admiral Cassian Vale

**Titles**: Lord of the Tides, Admiral of the Western Fleet, Warden of the Open Sea

### 🧔 Appearance & Reputation

Lord Admiral Cassian Vale is a **charismatic and sharp-minded ruler**, equally at ease on a ship's deck or in a council chamber.

**Physical Description**:
- Tall, dark-haired, impeccably dressed
- Moves with the confidence of someone used to command
- Always appears calm—even in crisis

**Reputation**: Popular with sailors, merchants, and foreign envoys alike.

### ⚓ Rule and Authority

Cassian Vale governs Tidecross through **command of the fleet and mastery of politics**.

**Responsibilities**:
- Oversees the kingdom's largest naval force
- Maintains trade security and anti-piracy patrols
- Balances merchant guild demands against royal authority

**Philosophy**:
> *"Control the harbor, and the city controls itself."*

Unlike inland lords, his power rests as much on ships as on stone.

### 🌊 Awareness of External Threats

Lord Vale is acutely aware of rising dangers:
- Eldemar's naval movements concern him deeply
- Reports of ships vanishing without wreckage are increasing
- Strange lights and unnatural storms have been logged offshore

### ⛪ Faith and Outlook

Lord Vale honors gods of:
- Sea
- Travel
- Fortune
- Protection

He believes fate favors those who act decisively—but never blindly.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Tidecross

Tidecross feels:
- **Alive** - Constantly moving and changing
- **Opportunistic** - Where fortunes are made and lost
- **On the edge of chaos** - Barely controlled energy

The docks are full, the taverns loud, and the ships keep sailing.

Yet beneath the noise, a question spreads quietly:

> *What happens when the tide brings something back instead of taking it away?*

---

## Narrative Role

Tidecross serves as:
- Gateway for foreign threats and information
- Hub for maritime adventures and intrigue
- Source of wealth and corruption
- First contact point for sea-borne horrors
- Political counterweight to the capital
