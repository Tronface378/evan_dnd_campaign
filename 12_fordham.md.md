# 🌾 Fordham - The Last Quiet Fields

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Fordham is a **small, weather-beaten farming village**, standing at the southernmost edge of Edmaris' settled lands. Beyond it, the roads thin, patrols grow scarce, and the world feels less certain. Fordham does not mark the heart of the kingdom—it marks **how far the kingdom dares to go**.

It is a place people pass through on the way to somewhere more dangerous...  
or return to when they hope the danger is behind them.

---

## 🏘️ The Village Itself

Fordham is compact and humble.

### Layout

- A handful of stone-and-timber homes clustered around a central green
- Low barns and fenced fields stretching outward in uneven patches
- A shallow river crossing—the "ford" that gives the village its name
- Dirt roads worn smooth by carts and livestock rather than traffic

**Defenses**: There are no walls. Only fences, hedges, and distance.

**At Night**: The village is lit by lanterns hung low and close together.

---

## 🌱 Life and Labor

Fordham survives through:

### Economy

- Subsistence farming
- Small grain and vegetable plots
- Livestock raised more for survival than trade

**Trade**: What surplus exists is sent north toward Millford or traded with passing caravans. Most families grow what they eat and store what they can.

**Wealth**: People here measure wealth in **full root cellars and healthy animals**, not coin.

---

## ⚖️ Law and Protection

Fordham has:

### Security

- No standing guard
- One sworn knight who visits irregularly
- A small militia made up of farmers and herders

### Response to Danger

When trouble comes, the villagers rely on:
- Early warning
- Fast evacuation
- Prayers that danger passes them by

**Reality**: For greater threats, messengers are sent north—though help does not always arrive in time.

---

## 🌫️ The Shadow of the South

Fordham sits uncomfortably close to:

### Nearby Threats

- Old roads no longer used
- Ruins better left unexplored
- The distant pull of places like the **Crypt of the Ancients**

### Strange Occurrences

Strange things sometimes wander close:
- Animals behaving incorrectly
- Lights flickering far from the fields
- Sounds carrying across empty land at night

**Response**: The villagers do not chase these mysteries. They close their doors and wait for morning.

---

## ⛪ Faith in Fordham

Shrines here are simple stone markers.

### Worship

- Gods of harvest, home, and protection are favored
- Offerings are bread, grain, and small carvings
- Prayer is quiet, private, and frequent

**Nature of Faith**: Faith here is not questioned—it is relied upon.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Fordham

Fordham feels:
- Peaceful on the surface
- Lonely beneath it
- Fragile when looked at too closely

The people are kind, cautious, and deeply tired of bad news.

### Common Saying

> *"If the fields still grow, the world hasn't ended."*

---

## 🌾 Mayor Rowan Hale

**Title**: Reeve of Fordham, Keeper of the Southern Fields

### 🧔 Appearance & Demeanor

Mayor Rowan Hale is an **older, soft-spoken man**, weathered more by worry than by age.

**Physical Description**:
- Thin, stooped posture from years of farm labor
- Graying hair kept short and unadorned
- Dresses like any other villager—patched coat, sturdy boots
- Often smells faintly of soil and grain

**Impression**: Does not look like a leader. That is precisely why the village trusts him.

### ⚖️ How He Governs

Rowan Hale governs Fordham with **care and restraint**.

**Approach**:
- Avoids confrontation unless absolutely necessary
- Settles disputes quietly and locally
- Prioritizes safety over pride

**Philosophy**:
> *"We don't win fights down here. We survive them."*

**Awareness**: Keeps meticulous mental track of:
- Which families are struggling
- Which fields failed last season
- Who hasn't slept well lately

**Reality**: Nothing in Fordham escapes his notice—he just doesn't speak of it unless he must.

### 🛡️ Authority and Limits

Mayor Hale commands:
- A small, informal militia of farmers and herders
- A rotating night watch during troubled weeks
- Messengers trained to ride north at the first sign of real danger

**Understanding**: Knows Fordham cannot stand against serious threats. His job is to **buy time**, not victory.

**Willingness**: When danger grows too large, Hale does not hesitate to ask for help—even if it means admitting fear.

### ⛪ Faith and Belief

Hale's faith is **quiet and stubborn**.

**Practices**:
- Honors gods of home, harvest, and protection
- Leaves offerings before dawn, alone
- Believes the gods listen more closely to whispered prayers

**Expectations**: Does not ask for miracles. He asks for mornings.

### 🌫️ The Mood Around Him

Under Mayor Hale's guidance, Fordham feels:
- Cautious
- Close-knit
- Permanently braced for loss

The villagers trust him because he never lies about danger—and never exaggerates it.

---

## 🔮 Narrative Role

Fordham works best as:
- A place to show what is at stake for ordinary people
- An early victim of southern horrors
- A quiet starting point or tragic waypoint
- A village that cannot survive prolonged attention from evil

Mayor Rowan Hale is:
- A grounding, human figure in a fragile place
- One of the first civilians to believe the party
- A reminder that leadership is often endurance, not command
- A man who will sacrifice his position, his reputation, and even his life to get his people out

> If Fordham survives the coming darkness, it will be because Rowan Hale knew **when to let go**.

**Stakes**: If Fordham falls, few beyond the locals will notice at first. But it will mean the darkness has moved **closer**.
