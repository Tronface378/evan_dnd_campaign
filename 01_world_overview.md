## The Kingdom of Edmaris

Edmaris is a kingdom that rose from **trade, order, and law**. It stands as a bastion of human civilization in a world where ancient threats are beginning to stir once more.

### Current Ruler

**King Alric III** - Known as a benevolent but just king who governs with fairness and wisdom.

**Court Magician**: Archmagus Selvaren Holt - The king's most trusted magical advisor.

---

## Current State of the World

### Rising Tensions

- **Eldemar Threat**: The neighboring nation of Eldemar covets Edmaris' natural resources and has become increasingly aggressive
- **Monster Attacks**: Frequency of monster attacks is increasing across all settlements
- **Resource Strain**: Strategic resources (food, metal, timber) are under pressure

### Security Structure

#### Major Cities
- Maintain full militias for protection
- Well-equipped and trained forces
- Regular patrols and strong defenses

#### Smaller Towns & Villages
- Small militias composed primarily of citizens
- **Heavy reliance on Guild support** for major threats
- Limited professional military presence

## The Guild System

Guilds form the backbone of Edmaris' response to extraordinary threats. They operate semi-independently but are regulated by the crown.

### The Adventurers Guild

**Function**: Mercenary force for dangerous tasks

**Compensation**:
- Payment typically from the quest requester
- Crown provides assistance for critical missions
- Per-job payment structure

**Services**:
- Monster hunting and elimination
- Dungeon exploration
- Escort and protection missions
- Tasks beyond normal citizens' capabilities

**Training Location**: Capitol city of Edmaris

### The Mages Guild (The Arcanum)

**Function**: Magic regulation and training

**Responsibilities**:
- Research and advancement of magical knowledge
- Training of wizards and sorcerers
- **Enforcement of magical law**

**Critical Rule**: Magic is a **licensed practice**
- All individuals with magical ability must either:
  - Undergo training and become licensed
  - Be permanently forbidden from using their abilities
- **Reporting Unlicensed Mages**: Every citizen's duty
- **Enforcement**: Citizens may turn in or kill unlicensed mages based on threat level

**Training Location**: [The Arcanum of Edmaris](02_arcanum.md) - The official magical college

### The Healers Guild

**Function**: Medical and magical healing training

**Requirements**:
- License **highly recommended** but not required
- Trains both magical and non-magical healers

**Training Location**: [Raven's Reach](mentioned) - The Holy Sanctuary

## Character Training Paths

### Arcane Casters (Wizards, Sorcerers, Warlocks)
**Location**: [The Arcanum of Edmaris](02_arcanum.md)
- Mandatory for all arcane magic users
- Binding oaths to the crown required
- Comprehensive magical education and regulation

### Divine Casters (Clerics, Paladins)
**Location**: Raven's Reach - The Holy Sanctuary
- Training in divine magic and healing
- Religious instruction
- Combat training for paladins

### Warriors & Martial Classes
**Two Options**:
1. **Local Training**: Apprentice with a master in your home town
2. **Guild Training**: Undergo formal training with the Adventurers Guild in the capitol

## The Gathering Storm

Beneath the surface of political tensions and monster attacks, something deeper stirs. Ancient ruins hold secrets better left buried. The gods themselves seem uncertain. And in the darkness, an elder evil (Tharizdun) begins its approach.

The question facing Edmaris is not whether darkness is coming—but whether the kingdom can stand when it arrives.
