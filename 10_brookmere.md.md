# 🌊 Brookmere - The Quiet Between Roads

[← Back to Index](README.md)

---

## Overview

Brookmere is a **small, tranquil town** built along the edge of a broad freshwater lake fed by clear streams and slow-moving brooks. It sits away from major trade arteries, connected to the wider kingdom by modest roads and regular river traffic rather than grand highways.

Where other towns brace for danger, Brookmere **hopes it will pass them by**.

---

## 🏘️ The Town of Water and Wood

Brookmere is laid out gently along the shoreline.

### Layout

- Wooden docks stretch into calm water
- Waterwheels and mills line the brooks feeding the lake
- Homes are timber-framed with stone foundations to resist flooding
- Reed boats, fishing skiffs, and ferries are more common than wagons

### Pace of Life

The town's pace is slow and deliberate. Bells mark time for work and rest, not alarms.

---

## 🌾 Livelihood and Trade

Brookmere survives on:

### Economy

- Fishing and freshwater trade
- Grain milling and flour exports
- River transport between nearby settlements

**Role**: Supplies food and river services rather than wealth or arms. During shortages elsewhere, Brookmere quietly becomes **more important than it appears**.

### Guild Presence

Guild presence is minimal:
- A small multi purpose guild hall
- Independent fishermen and millers
- Occasional adventurer contracts when the lake behaves strangely

---

## ⚖️ Law and Defense

Brookmere has:

### Security Forces

- No standing army
- A handful of sworn knights
- A small, locally trained watch

### Response to Threats

For threats beyond bandits or wildlife, Brookmere:
- Relies on guild aid
- Sends petitions to Edmaris
- Requests help from Raven's Reach when matters turn unnatural

**Reality**: The town is not defenseless—but it is not prepared for war.

---

## 🧑‍⚖️ Mayor Lysa Fenwick

### 🌿 Public Reputation

Mayor Lysa Fenwick is known as a **calm, thoughtful leader**, more mediator than ruler.

**Appearance**:
- Middle-aged, practical dress, always carries ledgers and letters
- Speaks gently but firmly
- Grew up in Brookmere and returned after years of river trade

**Philosophy**: Believes leadership is about **keeping people steady**, not inspiring them to greatness.

### 🧠 Governing Style

Lysa governs through:
- Consensus and careful planning
- Early problem-solving rather than crisis response
- Quiet cooperation with neighboring towns

**Core Belief**:
> *"Panic spreads faster than any flood."*

**Approach**: Cautious about calling for outside help, but never too proud to do so when lives are at stake.

---

## 🕯️ Faith in Brookmere

Shrines here honor gods of:
- Water and travel
- Harvest and sustenance
- Protection of the home

**Offerings**: Small, personal, and sincere.

---

## 🌫️ The Mood of Brookmere

Brookmere feels:
- Peaceful
- Cautious
- Increasingly fragile

The people want to believe the lake will always provide.

Mayor Fenwick is quietly preparing for the day it doesn't.

---

## 🔮 Narrative Role

Brookmere works best as:
- A peaceful respite between dangers
- A location vulnerable to water-based or nature-corrupted threats
