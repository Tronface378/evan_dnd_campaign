## Overview

Edmaris is a **city of stone, order, and faith**, built to endure rather than dazzle. It is not ancient like Suncrest nor carved like High Khuldar, but it is **deliberate**—every wall placed, every district planned, every road measured for control and defense.

At its heart rises the castle.

---

## 🏰 The Crownspire Castle

### The Heart of Edmaris

The **Crownspire** stands on a broad, elevated hill at the exact center of the city.

**Architecture**:
- Built of pale stone darkened by age and smoke
- Visible from every district
- Its central keep rises higher than any temple spire

### Functions

- Royal residence of King Alric III
- Seat of governance and law
- Final refuge in times of siege

### The Three Concentric Rings

1. **The Inner Ward**  
   - Nobles and royal advisors
   - Elite guard barracks
   - Private royal chambers

2. **The High Court Ring**  
   - Government ministries
   - Royal archives
   - Foreign embassies

3. **The Civic Ring**  
   - Guild headquarters
   - Major institutions
   - Administrative offices

**Visual**: From above, Edmaris looks like a **crown laid flat**, with the castle as its central gem.

---

## ⛪ The Temple District - Ring of the Gods

Encircling the inner wards lies the **Temple District**, a place of constant prayer and quiet rivalry.

### Features

- Grand temples to major deities line wide, processional avenues
- Lesser shrines fill side streets and plazas
- Bells, chants, and incense mark the passing hours

### Religious Balance

**No single faith dominates.**

Instead, Edmaris enforces **religious balance by law**, preventing any one temple from gaining dominance over others.

### Strategic Importance

This district is often the first to feel:
- Divine unrest
- Ominous visions
- Changes in miracles or divine favor

---

## 🏛️ The Guildward - Industry and Influence

Beyond the temples lies the **Guildward**, loud and alive.

### Features

- Massive guild halls of stone and timber
- Training yards and practice grounds
- Hiring boards and contract halls
- Taverns filled with mercenaries, scholars, and adventurers

### Economic Power

Here, coin moves faster than law—but always under watch.

**Key Principle**: Guilds are powerful, but never sovereign. Royal inspectors are always nearby.

### Major Guilds Present

- [The Adventurers Guild](01_world_overview.md#the-adventurers-guild)
- [The Mages Guild / Arcanum](02_arcanum.md)
- [The Healers Guild](01_world_overview.md#the-healers-guild)
- Merchants' Guilds
- Craftsmen's Guilds

## Current Mood

The capital feels:
- **Ordered** - Systems and structures are maintained
- **Watchful** - Authorities are alert to threats
- **Strained** - Resources and attention are stretched thin

As monster attacks increase and tensions with Eldemar grow, the capital serves as both the kingdom's brain and its shield.
